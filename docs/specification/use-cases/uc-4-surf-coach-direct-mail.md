# UC-4: Someone mails the surf coach directly

Status: draft

## Walkthrough

Mail arrives addressed to the surf coach's own address. Routing shortcuts on the address. The
persona replies as itself, in its own voice, from its own address. Directive decides whether the
reply goes out automatically or waits for approval.

## Awkward parts

- The mail is not about surfing.
- The sender does not know they are mailing an agent.
- The reply commits to something.
